How it works. Winds and temperatures come from the NOAA GFS model through Open-Meteo, at 400/350/300/250/200/150 hPa (about FL236/266/301/340/387/446). They are taken for each point at your estimated time overhead, and interpolated between those levels. For each level the tool computes TAS, ground speed and a fuel index over the cruise part of the route. The fuel index applies a generic Airbus-style off-optimum penalty: about 2% at 2000 ft and 5% at 4000 ft off OPT, matching published A320-family figures (you can change both in More settings). The "FMS levels" pick is the set of 4 levels whose straight-line interpolation (what the FMS does) best matches the full wind profile in your cruise band.

Limits. This is a planning cross-check, not certified performance data. It is not an approved EFB application. The FMS, OFP, ATC and REC MAX/buffet margins always govern. Ground track is great-circle between the points you enter, so add airway fixes for accuracy.
